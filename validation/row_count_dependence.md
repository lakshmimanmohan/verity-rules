## Group-aware row subsets

Candidate source columns (uniqueness ratio >= 0.5): email, phone, address, city, zip_code, SSN. Rows sharing a value in any of them (a value shared by 2 to 10 rows) are linked, and linked rows form a group that is kept or dropped whole. 1,847 groups: 586 single rows and 1,261 groups of 2 or more rows (largest 11, median among multi-row groups 3). Groups are shuffled with the seed and added whole while they fit the target row count; groups that do not fit are skipped, so the row count matches exactly.
